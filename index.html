The artist

Astro’ is short for Astronomical. The name is about the heights he’s taking this. Born Eric Wilson, raised in Lisbon, based in Nottingham, he bends between trapsoul, R&B and rap. He calls himself the Nighttime King because everything he makes is built for after dark — the nights with the room full and the volume up, or the ones that belong to two people.

The range comes from the house he grew up in. His sister played R&B and nothing else: D’Angelo, Marvin Gaye, R. Kelly, then Chris Brown, Summer Walker, PARTYNEXTDOOR. His brother played hip-hop — Lil Wayne, The Game, Snoop, Pac, Biggie. He took both. He says he isn’t doing anything sonically that nobody’s done before. What he can do is capture the essence of an album like PARTYNEXTDOOR’s P3, then turn around and drop a trap song on some Future and Thug vibes. He credits that upbringing for all of it.

It got serious after a Drake record. Hearing “Do Not Disturb” in the middle of a heartbreak did something to him, and he says he’s still working out what. He started recording in South Lisbon in 2022 on a phone app, through earphones from the corner shop. Jimba, one of his closest friends, backed it first. He doesn’t write. He boots up the beat and goes, finds the melody off the dome, and records alone, nothing prepared before the mic is on.

He’s been at it about three years, one of which he took off. The hard stretches were emotional more than financial, and the time away went into learning what his own sound was. He rates finding it above any number he’s put up since, and the numbers came anyway, all of it independent: over 600,000 streams on SoundCloud, 30,000 on Spotify. His debut mixtape WYNTER lands in October. Nine tracks, no features, all produced by fukkyurem, built on Toronto fundamentals — “the Trap’House is the new Toronto”, Trap’s house, his house. He wants it heard in the order he drops it. There’s a progression across the nine and it escalates.
